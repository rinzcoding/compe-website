CompE Guide - Certifications

CompTIA Network+

Issuer: CompTIA. Level: Beginner. Networking fundamentals. Good for network and IT support paths.

CompTIA Security+

Issuer: CompTIA. Level: Intermediate. Core cybersecurity knowledge for security-related roles.

Cisco CCNA

Issuer: Cisco. Level: Intermediate. Routing, switching, and network fundamentals, widely recognized in networking.

AWS Cloud Practitioner

Issuer: Amazon Web Services. Level: Beginner. Entry-level cloud computing concepts.

Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900)

Issuer: Microsoft. Level: Beginner. Cloud basics on Microsoft Azure.

PCEP / PCAP Python Certification

Issuer: Python Institute. Level: Beginner. Proves your Python programming skills.

Certified in Cybersecurity (CC)

Issuer: ISC2. Level: Beginner. Entry-level security certification aimed at newcomers.

Certified Computer Engineer (CCpE)

Issuer: Computer Engineering Certification Board (CpECB). Level: Beginner/Intermediate. Designed for Computer Engineering graduates with less than 10 years of professional practice to validate their foundational competency and early-career experience.

Professional Computer Engineer (PCpE)

Issuer: Computer Engineering Certification Board (CpECB). Level: Advanced. Geared toward established computer engineering practitioners with at least 10 years of professional experience to recognize advanced expertise and senior leadership in the tech industry.
