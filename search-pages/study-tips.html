CompE Guide - Study Tips

Code a little every day

Thirty minutes daily beats a six-hour cram once a week. Small habits build real skill.

Build projects, not just notes

Turn what you learn into something working: a blinking LED, a small app, a mini game.

Understand, don't memorize

Ask why a circuit or algorithm works. Understanding transfers to new problems; memorizing doesn't.

Master the math early

Calculus, algebra, and discrete math show up everywhere in computer engineering. Fix weak spots in Year 1.

Study with others

Explaining a topic to a classmate is one of the best ways to find what you don't understand yet.

Learn to read datasheets

Real engineers read documentation. Practice with an Arduino or sensor datasheet.

Use Git from day one

Save every project on GitHub. It becomes your portfolio by graduation.

Protect your sleep and health

Exams and debugging both go worse when you're exhausted. Rest is part of studying.
