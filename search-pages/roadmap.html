CompE Guide - Roadmap

Year 1: Build your foundation

Learn one programming language well (Python or C), refresh your math, and get comfortable with VS Code and Git. Make small projects like a calculator or a quiz game.

Year 2: Learn how hardware thinks

Study circuits and digital logic, then build real things: LED circuits, a simple Arduino project, and data structure practice in code. Start a GitHub portfolio.

Year 3: Go deeper and specialize

Take on embedded systems, networks, and operating systems. Join a club or competition, try an internship, and pick a direction such as embedded, networking, AI, or security.

Year 4: Show what you can do

Finish your capstone, polish your portfolio and resume, and practice for interviews. Consider one certification that matches your chosen direction.

After graduation: Launch your career

Apply for entry-level roles or further study. Check the licensure or professional registration requirements for computer engineers in your country.
